# Cold Email Templates Pack (B2B)

Prospecting emails that get replies. Personalize {FirstName}, {Company} and the icebreaker before sending.

## 1. The Short Prospecting Email
Subject: Quick question, {FirstName}
Hi {FirstName},
Noticed you work at {Company}. Quick question — are you handling {topic} internally?
Happy to share how we help companies like yours. 30 sec to look? 
Best,
{YourName}

## 2. The Meeting Hook
Subject: {Company} + growth
Hi {FirstName},
I saw {Company} is growing — congrats. We help teams like yours {outcome}. 
Open to a 15-min call this week?
— {YourName}

## 3. The Value-First (no pitch)
Subject: Free resource for {Company}
Hi {FirstName},
Here's a free {resource} I made for companies like {Company}. No strings.
If it helps, I'd love to chat.
— {YourName}

## 4. The Follow-Up (day 3)
Subject: Re: {topic}
Hi {FirstName},
Just bumping this — totally get you're busy. If now's not the time, no worries.
Worth a quick look?
— {YourName}

## 5. The Breakup Email (day 7)
Subject: Closing the loop
Hi {FirstName},
I'll assume it's not a fit for now. If things change, my door's open.
Wishing {Company} the best.
— {YourName}

## 6. The Customer-Reference
Subject: {Company} + {mutual}
Hi {FirstName},
{Name} at {mutual} mentioned you. They use us and love it — thought you'd want to see.
— {YourName}

## 7. The "I did research" Email
Subject: Idea for {Company}
Hi {FirstName},
I dug into {Company} and noticed {specific detail}. Here's a thought on {outcome}.
Would love your take.
— {YourName}

## 8. The Case-Study Based
Subject: How {Company} could save {X}
Hi {FirstName},
One client like {Company} saved {X} with {solution}. Case study attached.
Worth a peek?
— {YourName}

## 9. The Abandoned-Cart Style (retention)
Subject: Still open?
Hi {FirstName},
You left before finishing {previous step}. Want me to keep your spot?
— {YourName}

## 10. The Event/Webinar Invite
Subject: Free webinar for {Company}
Hi {FirstName},
We're running a free session on {topic} for {Company}-style teams. Limited spots.
Want the link?
— {YourName}

---

## Anti-Spam Checklist (protect your domain)
- Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC before sending.
- Warm up your domain (send 10-20/day for 2 weeks).
- Never send to unverified/invalid emails (bounces hurt your reputation).
- Keep the email under 200 words and avoid spammy words.
- Send between Tue-Thu, 8-11am local time.
- Follow up 2-3 times max, then move on.
